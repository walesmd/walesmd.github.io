The Shattered Crown

A top-down 2D adventure in the spirit of A Link to the Past, set in the world of Eldoria. Built with Phaser 3, and every piece of it — the pixel art, the chiptune music — is generated by code rather than drawn or recorded. It runs entirely in the browser, and offline.
